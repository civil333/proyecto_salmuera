# TECHNICAL NOTE: REQUEST FOR CLARIFICATIONS TO MITIGATION PLAN

## 1. CONTEXT AND PURPOSE

### 1.1 Background

On May 14, 2026 ADASA escalated in writing two contractual concerns to BW Water:

- Conflict between the Fedco Estimated Arrival in Penang (EAP) and the EX Works Shipping milestone for the three Fedco items (BH-09-001 High Pressure Pump, SIP-09-001 Feed Turbocharger, SIP-09-002 Interstage Turbocharger).
- Unannounced schedule variance of +25 days on PO date and +16 days on EAP detected between consecutive weekly trackers (SEMANA 04-05-26 → SEMANA 11-05-26), without formal communication from BW Water.

The original deadline for written confirmation of a mitigation plan was set at end of business Friday May 22, 2026.

### 1.2 BW Water Response

On Sunday May 24, 2026 at 18:20 local time, approximately two days after the requested deadline, BW Water released the following set of documents:

- 25007 Taltal — Mitigation Plan (PDF, signed by Eduardo Yamauchi, PMO Leader)
- 12803 TALTAL Water Treatment Plant Project — Recovery Schedule Updated 2026.05.22 (PDF)
- Cover email from Eduardo Yamauchi summarizing six mitigation actions

### 1.3 Purpose of this Technical Note

This Note is not a formal acceptance, rejection, or conditional acceptance of the mitigation plan. ADASA reserves its formal position until the technical clarifications requested in Section 3 are received, reviewed, and validated against the Technical Specification, Inspection and Testing Base Plan, and Technical Offer documents.

Each clarification requested below is tied to a specific statement, assumption, or unilateral change introduced by BW Water in the mitigation plan or recovery schedule. Items not addressed in this Note remain under separate review and are not waived by their omission here.

## 3. TECHNICAL CLARIFICATIONS REQUESTED

The mitigation plan is structured around six action items. This Section requests clarifications on each item in the same order.

### 3.1 Fedco FAT Execution Plan

BW Water states that the vendor is being pushed to complete fabrication before July 29, 2026, with early airfreight immediately after manufacturing completion. The arrival in Penang is described by BW Water as "expected around 10 August" and qualified as "based on current estimation".

ADASA requests the following clarifications:

**1.A — Firm commitment supporting the 29-Jul-2026 fabrication date.** Indicate whether 29-Jul-2026 represents a firm commitment of Fedco supported by written acknowledgment (PO confirmation, signed schedule, vendor letter), or remains an internal estimation by BW Water. Provide the supporting document.

**1.B — Pre-EX Works technical documentation delivery schedule for the three Fedco items.** The shipping arrangement BW Water to ADASA is EX Works Penang; ADASA arranges the international transport from Penang to Taltal. Provide a delivery schedule with firm dates for the following items prior to EX Works on the latest date declared by BW Water (16-Aug-2026, conditional to the Fedco arrival date reconciled per clarification 4.A): final packing list, as-built drawings, material certificates and test reports, FAT reports executed at the Fedco facility, operation and maintenance manuals, and approved equipment datasheets.

**1.C — Uncertainty range and worst-case date for the BW Water statement "around 10 August".** Provide the documented worst-case arrival date in Penang and the conditions under which the BW Water estimation would move further to the right.

**1.D — Backup plan in the event Fedco arrival in Penang slips beyond the BW Water declared date.** Provide a documented contingency plan (decoupling option analogous to the CIP Pump approach in the next subsection, additional expediting measures, partial alternative vendor, or other). An open response stating that mitigation is under review will not be considered a substantive answer.

### 3.2 CIP Pump Mitigation

BW Water states that the CIP flushing pump would be treated as a separate major item and excluded from the main UHPRO shipment, conditioned by BW Water to the event the lead time exceeds 11 weeks, with individual FAT executed at the vendor manufacturing facility and direct shipment to site.

ADASA requests the following clarifications:

**2.A — Current CIP vendor lead time, contractual basis of the 11-week threshold, and decoupling decision deadline.** State the actual lead time of the CIP pump vendor as of May 25, 2026. Identify the contractual basis of the 11-week threshold proposed by BW Water as the decoupling trigger. The threshold does not appear in the Technical Specification, the Technical Offer, or the BAE. If the lead time already exceeds 11 weeks under the BW Water criterion, the decoupling condition is determined and shall be formalized. Provide the date by which BW Water will release the decoupling decision in writing.

**2.B — INCOTERM applicable to the direct-to-site CIP shipment.** Define whether the direct shipment to Taltal is under DDP (BW Water responsible for transport and customs clearance to site) or EX Works at the CIP vendor facility (ADASA arranges transport). Provide the complete pre-shipping technical documentation required by ADASA to plan logistics: packing list, dimensional drawings, material certificates, FAT report, operation manual.

**2.C — Protocol of the individual FAT at the CIP vendor facility.** Submit the FAT procedure with acceptance criteria, including motor test, hydrostatic test, performance test, balance test, and RTD records as committed in the BW Water Technical Offer — Inspection and Testing Plan (Section 12). Confirm the ADASA witness right at the vendor facility with at least one month notice in advance, consistent with the Technical Specification — Inspections During Manufacturing (Section 8).

**2.D — Quantified impact on site personnel and supervision scope.** If the CIP pump arrives directly to site without integrated FAT in Penang, the equivalent tests at site are no longer Factory Acceptance Tests but become Site Acceptance Tests. Provide:

- Estimated additional days of BW Water personnel at site to execute SAT on the CIP loop, beyond the supervision window defined in BAE 12803 and the Economic Offer.
- Position on cost allocation for the additional personnel days: BW Water absorbing them at no cost to ADASA, or BW Water intending to invoice the additional days. ADASA does not anticipate absorbing additional personnel costs derived from unilateral decoupling decisions and reserves the right to address cost allocation once the SAT impact is quantified by BW Water.
- The same logic applies to any other equipment that BW Water proposes to decouple from the main Penang skid.

### 3.3 UHPRO Skid Dry Testing

BW Water states that a dry testing of the UHPRO skid will be conducted prior to shipment to "substantially verify system integration and functionality", with detailed procedure to be shared "as soon as possible in June". BW Water also states that dry testing minimizes "potential commissioning risks during site activities". ADASA understands this last statement as implying that part of the original FAT scope is transferred to site, and requests confirmation in the clarifications below.

ADASA requests the following clarifications:

**3.A — Detailed FAT scope coverage in the dry test.** Submit a table that specifies, for each sub-system (high-pressure piping, controls, instrumentation, electrical, motors, low-pressure piping, valves), which acceptance items are executed in dry testing and which items are deferred to Site Acceptance Test. The Performance and Running Test committed for the High Pressure Pump and turbochargers in the BW Water Technical Offer — Inspection and Testing Plan (Section 12) shall be explicitly identified in the table.

**3.B — Dry test procedure delivery and ADASA approval window.** BW Water shall submit the detailed dry test procedure in writing no later than **June 15, 2026**. ADASA review window will be 5 business days from receipt of the complete procedure. Dry testing shall not start at the Penang Workshop until ADASA has issued written approval of the procedure.

**3.C — Integrated FAT location and timing (with Fedco equipment installed).** The recovery schedule shows the FAT activity from 27-Jul to approximately 10-Aug-2026, executed without the Fedco equipment present at site. The window between the Fedco arrival in Penang declared by BW Water and the EX Works shipment (16-Aug-2026) is approximately six days under the most favorable arrival date, less under the worst-case date that BW Water has not yet provided per clarification 1.C. Indicate whether the integrated Factory Acceptance Test, with Fedco equipment installed and the system running under load, is executed in Penang within that window, or transferred to site in Taltal. If transferred to site, the analysis under clarification 2.D applies (SAT instead of FAT, extension of personnel days, cost allocation).

**3.D — Location of the Performance and Running Test committed in the Technical Offer.** The BW Water Technical Offer — Inspection and Testing Plan (Section 12) commits a Performance and Running Test for the High Pressure Pump and turbochargers. This test requires the system running under hydraulic load and cannot be executed in dry conditions. Indicate where the test is executed: in Penang post-Fedco arrival (within the six-day window described above), or at site Taltal. If at site, submit the written test procedure, confirm ADASA witness presence, define acceptance criteria, and define a re-test window in case of failure.

### 3.4 EX Works Shipping Schedule Deferral

BW Water states that the EX Works shipment date is revised from August 3, 2026 to August 16, 2026 to accommodate integration activities following the Fedco arrival in Penang, which BW Water characterizes as "currently estimated on 8 August" in the Mitigation Plan document.

ADASA requests the following clarifications:

**4.A — Internal inconsistency between BW Water documents regarding the Fedco arrival date.** The cover email of May 24, 2026 states the Fedco arrival in Penang as "around 10 Aug", while the Mitigation Plan document states "currently estimated on 8 August". The two-day discrepancy is internal to the BW Water document set released on May 22, 2026 and shall be reconciled. State in writing the single firm reference date for the Fedco arrival in Penang.

**4.B — Pre-shipment Hold Point status at May 25, 2026.** Per the Technical Specification — Engineering and Documentation to Develop (Section 7) and the Technical Specification — Minimum Scope of FAT (Section 8.1), three items of documentation shall be available and approved by ADASA as a prerequisite to authorize dispatch of the module. These are: SEC compliance verification (Hold Point per Section 8.1); fabrication quality dossier with Super Duplex material certificates (per Section 7); and the Maritime Transport Preservation, Packing and Preparation Procedure (per Section 7). State the current status of each item and the firm delivery date prior to EX Works on August 16, 2026.

**4.C — Formalization of the downstream cascade in the next weekly tracker.** The cascade derived from the EX Works deferral affects site delivery (approximately September 27, 2026), site supervision start, training (approximately November 11, 2026), and contractual close-out (approximately November 16, 2026). Confirm whether BW Water intends to formalize these new dates in the next weekly tracker as the updated contractual baseline, or whether they remain internal estimates. Without formalization, no reference exists for the detection of future schedule variances.

### 3.5 Alternative RO Cartridge Filter

BW Water proposes an alternative RO cartridge filter with a configuration change from horizontal to vertical and a material change from FRP to SS316, "with shorter lead time" and "while maintaining compliance". ADASA notes that "maintaining compliance" is the precise point under verification in the clarifications below.

ADASA requests the following clarifications:

**5.A — Material justification of SS316 against the operating salinity of the filter fluid.** SS316 (and SS316L) has a pitting resistance equivalent number (PREN) of approximately 26, which is widely documented as marginal for chloride concentrations above 20,000 ppm and susceptible to pitting and crevice corrosion in concentrated brine service. The project material standard for high-pressure piping per the Technical Specification — Process Piping (Section 5.2) is Super Duplex UNS S32750 with PREN above 40. Justify why SS316 is selected over SS316L, duplex, or super duplex with PREN above 40, and provide a technical compatibility analysis with the actual operating fluid (feed brine cartridge filter or CIP solution cartridge filter, as applicable).

**5.B — Complete datasheet of the alternative cartridge filter.** Submit the vendor name and model, dimensional drawing, complete operating conditions, material certificates, retention rating, design pressure, allowable differential pressure, design flow capacity, and projected cartridge replacement frequency.

**5.C — Updated as-built drawing of the container with the vertical filter configuration.** The horizontal-to-vertical change alters the container internal layout, including footprint, operator access for cartridge replacement, and vertical clearance for cartridge removal. Submit the updated as-built drawing of the container with the new configuration for ADASA review.

**5.D — ADASA pre-approval process before BW Water places the Purchase Order.** Acknowledge in writing that BW Water will not place the Purchase Order for the alternative filter until ADASA has reviewed the documentation listed in 5.A, 5.B, and 5.C and released a formal position. ADASA review window will be 5 business days from receipt of the complete documentation.

**5.E — Process tie-ins immovability.** The configuration change shall not alter the position, height, or orientation of the container interface connections towards the existing plant: feed brine inlet, permeate outlet, reject outlet, drainage outlets, chemical supply lines, and service lines. Submit written attestation that the tie-ins remain identical to the approved design, with reference to the corresponding tie-in drawing.

### 3.6 RO Pressure Vessels — ASME Certification

BW Water states that the vendor has proposed fabrication of the RO Pressure Vessels without ASME stamp certification, "while still maintaining fabrication works and testing in accordance with ASME standards". The mitigation plan indicates that the ASME stamp adds approximately six weeks to the lead time.

ADASA notes that Purchase Order M184 was placed by BW Water with Protec Arisawa Europe S.A. on 20-Apr-2026 for the Pressure Vessels (model BPV81200SP), with an Estimated Arrival in Penang declared by the vendor of 29-May-2026 (a lead time of approximately 5.5 weeks). Between that arrival date and the revised EX Works on 16-Aug-2026, the buffer in the schedule exceeds 10 weeks. Under these timings, the six-week impact attributed to the ASME stamp in the mitigation plan does not exhaust the available margin and does not, by itself, justify removing the stamp from the originally offered scope.

ADASA further notes that the unpriced Purchase Order M184 was delivered to ADASA and forms part of the seven major equipment items that support the payment milestone EP-2 under BAE Clause 31. Any unilateral modification of the original scope of M184, particularly the removal of the "Test certification to ASME X" committed in the BW Water Technical Offer Inspection and Testing Plan (Section 12), constitutes a change to the contracted scope. The change requires a formal Change Order and may affect the eligibility of M184 as a conforming instrument for EP-2.

ADASA requests the following clarifications. Items 6.C, 6.D, and 6.E are tied to specific articles of the Technical Specification and to BAE Clause 31, which set the conditions under which any change in certification method may be evaluated.

**6.A — Pre-existing inconsistency in the Technical Offer.** The BW Water Technical Offer — Inspection and Testing Plan (Section 12, page 100) commits "Test certification to ASME X" with a pressure rating stated as "1000 PSI for SWRO" and "300 PSI BWRO". The actual operating system is UHPRO at 1800 psi as specified in the Technical Specification — Pressure Vessels (Section 5.1.6). ASME Section X has a documented practical ceiling near 1500 psi for FRP vessels, which makes the originally offered rating insufficient to cover UHPRO operation regardless of stamp method. Clarify formally whether the originally offered ASME X certification was valid for 1800 psi operation. If it was not, the Technical Offer contains a pre-existing inconsistency that shall be resolved on its own terms, separate from any current schedule discussion.

**6.B — Formal vendor quotation with two options.** Submit a formal letter from **Protec Arisawa Europe S.A.** (vendor of Purchase Order M184) presenting two options: (i) with ASME X stamp — lead time, maximum certifiable rating, cost; (ii) without ASME stamp — lead time, cost, declared operating rating. The current statement that the omission of the stamp shortens the lead time by six weeks shall be evidenced by vendor documentation, given that the current vendor-declared EAP Penang of 29-May-2026 leaves a buffer of more than 10 weeks against the revised EX Works on 16-Aug-2026.

**6.C — Updated Detailed Inspection and Testing Plan as a prerequisite to fabrication.** Per the Technical Specification — Engineering Submittals (Section 7), any change in the certification method of Pressure Vessels constitutes a modification of the Detailed Inspection and Testing Plan. The Specification states that *"written approval of the Detailed Inspection and Testing Plan by ADASA is an indispensable requirement to authorize the start of fabrication of major components"*. ADASA requires that the updated Detailed Inspection and Testing Plan be submitted and approved in writing prior to start of Pressure Vessel fabrication under the modified certification method. Submit the updated Detailed Inspection and Testing Plan for the Pressure Vessels and state BW Water's plan to comply with this prerequisite.

**6.D — ADASA witness right at the Protec Arisawa Europe vendor facility.** Per the Technical Specification — Inspections During Manufacturing (Section 8), all equipment is subject to inspection by ADASA prior to dispatch, with notification at least one month in advance. ADASA will exercise the witness right at the Protec Arisawa Europe vendor facility for the following tests: hydrostatic test at the operating rating of 1800 psi, non-destructive examination, dimensional verification, and material verification. Confirm the proposed inspection window and the documentation that will be made available to ADASA inspectors.

**6.E — Linkage to the BAE Clause 31 payment milestone.** Per the Technical Specification — Minimum Scope of FAT (Section 8.1), the Factory Acceptance Test, including documented hydrostatic tests, is a prerequisite for module dispatch authorization and serves as the support for the payment milestone defined in BAE Clause 31. Acknowledge BW Water's understanding that, without an equivalent certification, the approved updated Detailed Inspection and Testing Plan, and the witnessed Factory Acceptance Test, the payment milestone associated with dispatch remains conditioned.
