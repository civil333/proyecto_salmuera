# TECHNICAL NOTE: DESIGNATION OF THIRD-PARTY SHOP INSPECTOR AND INSPECTION SCHEDULE

## 1. CONTEXT AND PURPOSE

### 1.1 Background

The Second Stage RO Brine Module is being fabricated at the BW Water workshop in Penang, Malaysia. Under the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37 — Quality, Inspection and Testing, and Clause 37.1 — Third-Party Inspector Intervention), ADASA holds the right, at its own discretion and cost, to designate an independent and qualified inspection agency to perform or witness, on ADASA's behalf, the inspections, verifications and tests defined in the approved Inspection and Test Plan and in the Technical Specification, including the Factory Acceptance Test.

The governing acceptance document is the Inspection and Test Plan (P22-BA-09-000-004), Rev 0, approved by ADASA (Code 1). Its Hold Points (H) and Witness Points (W) assigned to ADASA / third party define the inspection interventions addressed by this Note.

### 1.2 Purpose of this Technical Note

This Note formally: (a) designates Bureau Veritas as ADASA's Third-Party Inspector for the shop fabrication and Factory Acceptance Test of the module, stating its identity and general scope as required by the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37.1); (b) communicates the planned shop inspection schedule so that BW Water can align its Hold Point and Witness Point notifications accordingly; and (c) requests BW Water to confirm the corresponding notification calendar under the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37.2).

This Note is a notification. It does not modify the approved Inspection and Test Plan, does not waive any Hold Point, Witness Point or acceptance right of ADASA, and does not relieve BW Water of any of its contractual obligations. The inspection windows communicated in the next Section are indicative and are to be confirmed against the formal Hold Point and Witness Point notifications that BW Water shall issue for each activity.

## 3. THIRD-PARTY INSPECTOR DESIGNATION AND INSPECTION SCHEDULE

### 3.1 Designation of the Third-Party Inspector

Under the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37.1 — Third-Party Inspector Intervention), ADASA hereby notifies BW Water of the following designation:

- **Third-Party Inspector:** Bureau Veritas.
- **General scope:** to perform and witness, on behalf of ADASA, the inspections, verifications and tests assigned to ADASA / third party in the approved Inspection and Test Plan and required by the Technical Specification, during the fabrication and the Factory Acceptance Test of the module at the Penang workshop. This comprises quality-assurance surveillance during fabrication (material identification, welding and non-destructive examination, dimensional control, hydrostatic testing, coating, electrical assembly) and the witnessing of the Factory Acceptance Test through the Dispatch Release.
- **Authority:** the Third-Party Inspector acts as ADASA's representative for attendance to Hold Points and Witness Points and for the issuance of inspection records. Its participation or absence at any stage does not relieve BW Water of any of its contractual obligations, consistent with the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37.1).

### 3.2 Planned Shop Inspection Schedule

The inspection campaign is organized as six weekly quality-surveillance visits during fabrication, followed by the witnessing of the Factory Acceptance Test. The windows below are indicative and reflect the BW Water Fabrication Schedule dated 01-Jul-2026; the effective attendance days will be fixed against the Hold Point and Witness Point notifications requested in Section 4. Inspection points are identified by their description and by their designation in the approved Inspection and Test Plan (H = Hold Point, W = Witness Point).

| Visit | Indicative window (2026) | Fabrication scope and key inspection points | Contractual basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 27–31 Jul | Super Duplex and SS316 spool fabrication; Positive Material Identification on Super Duplex (W); welder / procedure qualification; visual inspection of Super Duplex welds (W); critical material receipt | Technical Specification — Inspections During Manufacturing; BAE Clause 37 |
| 2 | 3–7 Aug | Spool fabrication completion; non-destructive examination — RT, PT (root and final) and UT of high-pressure welds (W); dimensional control of skid (W) | Technical Specification — Inspections During Manufacturing (NDE Plan); BAE Clause 37 |

| 4 | 17–21 Aug | Structure coating completion; final piping and instrument assembly on skid (W); final coating DFT (W) | Technical Specification — Inspections During Manufacturing; BAE Clause 37 |

Notes on the schedule:

- The first Visit window opens on 27 July 2026, while Super Duplex welding is scheduled to start on 16 July 2026. BW Water shall sequence the Positive Material Identification and welding Witness Points so that they fall within an inspection window, or make the corresponding fabrication records available for review.
- The final block (Visit 6 and the Factory Acceptance Test) depends on the arrival at Penang of the High Pressure Pump and turbochargers (currently forecast around 2 September 2026) and of the PLC/LCP panel (currently forecast at Penang around 28 August 2026, per the BW Water Notice of Delay dated 7 July 2026). Any further movement of these dates will shift Visit 6 and the FAT witnessing accordingly, which is the reason the schedule is anchored to the Hold Point and Witness Point notifications rather than to fixed calendar dates.

### 3.3 Notification and Coordination Protocol

Consistent with the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37.2 — Coordination and Attendance to Intervention Points):

- BW Water shall formally notify ADASA of the scheduled dates for each Hold Point and Witness Point of the approved Inspection and Test Plan, observing the minimum advance notice established in the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37): thirty (30) days for tests and inspections, consistent with the one-month advance notice required by the Technical Specification (Inspections During Manufacturing).
- ADASA will confirm the intention to attend (directly or through the Third-Party Inspector) or to waive attendance within three (3) business days of the notification.
- For Hold Points, BW Water shall not proceed with the subsequent activity without the presence and/or written authorization of ADASA or its representative. For Witness Points, BW Water may proceed on the scheduled date if ADASA does not respond within the three-business-day window.

### 3.4 Access and Facilities

Under the Bases Administrativas Especiales (Clause 37.1), BW Water shall grant the Third-Party Inspector the same level of access to its facilities, and to those of its relevant subcontractors, as granted to ADASA's direct representatives, together with unrestricted and timely access to the documentation, records, equipment and personnel required to carry out the inspection activities, at no additional cost to ADASA.

## 5. ADASA RESERVATIONS

The designation of the Third-Party Inspector and the communication of this schedule do not waive any Hold Point, Witness Point or acceptance right of ADASA, and do not relieve BW Water of any of its contractual obligations. No inspection point is waived by its omission from the indicative schedule in this Note.
