# 1. Executive Summary

**TRANSMITTAL VERDICT: 3 — To be revised.** Submittals 25007-0096, 25007-0097, 25007-0099 and 25007-0100, eight documents, counting the lifting frame drawing that travelled inside the calculation addendum. Tally: 4 Code 1, 1 Code 2, 3 Code 3, no Code 4.

**Disposition at a glance:**

- **GA of Module Lifting from Top (yoke) Rev A — Code 3.** Re-issue as Rev B, complete in its three sheets and fit for fabrication, as a document of its own, in national welded IN or HN sections, with the splices and corner joints detailed and at least one intermediate beam at mid-length.
- **UHPRO Structural Calculation Report - Addendum Rev A — Code 3.** Re-issue under its own document code with the design of the lifting frame, including its torsion, racking and the buckling of the long members, under the NCh3171 combinations as a minimum, and the local check of the corner fittings.
- **Maintenance Lifting Points Layout and Details Rev A — Code 3.** Re-issue as Rev B with the runway beam or fixed lifting points for the pumps and both turbochargers.
- **GA of Antiscalant Dosing Tank Rev E — Code 2.** Set the level marking at 34 inches (864 mm) at Rev 0.
- **Piping Layout Rev E — Code 1.** Issue at Rev 0 with any routing change of the spools modified in September.
- **Valve List Rev 0, PLC/LCP FAT Procedure - Hardware Rev 0 and HMI Display Screenshot Rev 0 — Code 1.** No action.

**Why Code 3:**

- The yoke drawing requested in ADASA's e-mail of 15 September 2026, due on Thursday 17 September, arrived as sheet 1 of 3 inside the calculation addendum. It is a general arrangement that cannot be fabricated from: imported W10x49 sections, no intermediate beam, no splice for the 12,776 mm members, no corner joints and no weights.
- The addendum checks only the lugs. The frame, its stability during the lift and the corner fittings carry no design check.
- The maintenance lifting points sheet shows the lift of one motor, with no runway beam or fixed point, and leaves out the turbochargers.

Section 3 lists the pending observations from previous transmittals.

## 2.1 GA of Module Lifting from Top (yoke) Rev A — P22-DWG-09-005-019

**Response Code: 3 — To be revised**

**Status.** This drawing travelled as page 13 of the calculation addendum, outside the submittal form. ADASA's e-mail of 15 September 2026, subject "RE: TALTAL - Lifting package for the module and the scope of the structural review", asked for a yoke drawing to fabricate from by Thursday 17 September. It was to show dimensions, connections and welds, sling attachment points and the connection to the top corner fittings. The sheet received is a general arrangement. It states no weight, and the slings carry neither design tension nor length. The frame has no intermediate beam, and the torsion of the frame during the lift and the buckling of the 12,776 mm long members, compressed by the inclined slings, are not verified. Those long members show no splice, and there are no corner joint details. It is sheet 1 of 3, and sheets 2 and 3 were not received. The frame is drawn in W10x49, an imported rolled section with no steel grade stated, while ADASA fabricates the yoke in Chile and the approved Design Criteria require structural steel to NCh203. Note 2 leaves the connection at the corner fittings open. Itemised in P22-DWG-09-005-019_A_GA_Module_Lifting_from_Top_CC_ADASA.pdf.

**Action — re-issue as Rev B:** state the lifted weight of the module, the weight of the frame and rigging, the total load on the hook and the design tension and length of each sling leg (OBS-01). Add at least one intermediate beam at mid-length of the yoke, with its connections and welds, and verify the frame against torsion, racking and buckling in the calculation addendum (OBS-02). Detail the splice of the long members and the corner joints, with their welds (OBS-03). Submit the drawing complete, sheets 1 to 3, as a document of its own (OBS-04). Make each member the national welded equivalent, IN or HN series, with its steel grade to NCh203 (OBS-05). Show how each sling connects to its corner fitting (OBS-06). The Technical Specification (P22-ET-09-000-001-0), Section 7, page 29, requires the yoke drawing and the lifting drawing with weights. Page 30 of the same section does not allow the module to be released for transport until they are approved.

## 2.2 UHPRO Structural Calculation Report - Addendum Rev A — P22-CD-09-005-003

**Response Code: 3 — To be revised**

**Status.** The addendum carries the code of the UHPRO Structural Design Criteria and three different revision marks. It designs the two lugs of the lifting frame for the 9.5 t working load limit of the Crosby G-209 shackle, with the factor of safety of 2.0 and the 5 per cent lateral load of section F of those approved Design Criteria. That design is accepted. It is an allowable stress design on the shackle working load with no stated load combination, and the frame members, splices and joints carry no design check. The ISO corner fittings that take the sling loads are not checked either, although their local check was requested in ADASA's e-mail of 11 September 2026. The STAAD model of the frame is used only for the critical sling tension, and no check covers the torsion of the frame, its racking or the buckling of its long members. Itemised in P22-CD-09-005-003_A_Lifting_Addendum_CC_ADASA.pdf.

**Action — re-issue under its own document code:** issue the addendum under its own ADASA code, with one revision and one date (OBS-01). Design the frame members, splices and joints with the national IN or HN section, stating the method, under the NCh3171 combinations of Section D of the approved Design Criteria as a minimum and the 1.35D and 2.0D lifting factors of its Section F (OBS-02). Add the local check of the corner fittings and corner posts with the factor of safety of 2.0 and the 5 per cent lateral load (OBS-03). Model the frame with at least one intermediate beam at mid-length, and show that its torsion, its racking and the buckling of the long members stay within the limits of the method used (OBS-04). The Technical Specification, Section 7, page 29, requires the design of the yoke.
