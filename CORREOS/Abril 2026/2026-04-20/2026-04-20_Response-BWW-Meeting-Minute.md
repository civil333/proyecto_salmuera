**Subject:** Project 12803 — Taltal SWRO | ADASA Response to BW Water Meeting Minute of 20-Apr-2026

Eduardo,

Thank you for today's meeting minute. Three points need a formal ADASA position before BW Water advances on the outstanding decisions.

**1. Valve procurement.** No restriction on supplier nationality exists in the BAE or in the ET — Chinese, European or any international origin is acceptable. Compliance is measured against the ET Piping and Valves specification: body material ASTM A182 F53 (UNS S32750, PREN > 40), ANSI/ASME B16.5 Class 900 for the high-pressure circuit, PMI on 10% of Super Duplex components, NDE per ASME B31.3, and ADASA witness point at factory. Practical note: the Valve List Rev D of 18 March 2026 lists every brand, make and model as TBA — no supplier is formally declared yet. Once BW Water identifies the supplier, submit the manufacturer's datasheet, material certificates and inspection plan, and ADASA will evaluate within the standard contractual review.

**2. Operating Weights.** The table removed from the Equipment Layout Rev C advance copy (seven items, 34,739 lb / 15,758 kg in Rev A) must be restored in the next formal submittal. It is required for foundation design, NCh 2369 Zone 3 seismic verification and the module lifting plan. Deliver together with the Piping Layout final committed in your minute for the next 24 hours.

**3. Container doors.** The ET Container section requires four door provisions: a personnel door of **900 × 2,200 mm**, an equipment door sized to the largest installed equipment (110° opening, outward-swinging), an emergency door, and a **lateral sliding door**. Please confirm each of the four provisions in the Equipment Layout formal submittal, with dimensions per ET.

This note complements the Weekly Procurement Reporting Protocol sent earlier today. Any difference of interpretation, please flag it by return email before the first weekly tracker on Monday, 27 April 2026.
