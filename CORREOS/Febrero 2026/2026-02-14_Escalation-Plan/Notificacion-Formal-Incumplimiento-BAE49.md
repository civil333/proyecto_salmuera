## 1. PURPOSE

In accordance with Clause 49 of the Bases Administrativas Especiales (BAE) for Public Tender 12803, ADASA hereby formally notifies BW Water Americas Inc. of the non-compliance situations described below, and requests that BW Water remedy the identified breaches within **ten (10) calendar days** from receipt of this notification.

This notification is issued without prejudice to the automatic application of delay penalties under BAE Clause 43.1, which have been accruing since the engineering delivery deadline was exceeded on January 5, 2026.

## 3. IDENTIFIED NON-COMPLIANCE

### 3.1 Engineering Delay (BAE 43.1.a / ET Section 7)

The Technical Specifications (ET P22-ET-09-000-001-0, Section 7) require that all engineering documentation be submitted within **90 calendar days** of the award notification. BW Water's own baseline schedule (October 2025) established the engineering completion date as **January 5, 2026**.

As of the date of this notification, BW Water has not completed the engineering phase. BW Water's updated schedule (February 9, 2026) acknowledges that engineering will extend to **April 24, 2026**, representing a delay of **109 calendar days** beyond the original baseline.

**Current status of engineering deliverables:**
| Category | Count |
|----------|-------|
| Documents delivered and approved | 21 |
| Documents approved with notes | 14 |
| Documents requiring revision (verdict "3") | 13 |
| Documents rejected (verdict "4") | 3 |
| Documents never delivered | 14 |
| **Total identified in ET Section 7** | **~65** |

**Key undelivered documents (partial list):**
- MCC Datasheet (83+ days overdue from baseline)
- Modbus TCP Memory Map (committed in Transmittal N2 response, 45+ days overdue)
- A/C system n+1 configuration and thermal calculation (45+ days overdue)
- CIP Pump Datasheet (BH-09-002)
- Seismic Calculation Report
- Piping Flexibility Analysis

### 3.2 Failure to Comply with ADASA Review Process

BW Water has procured, manufactured, or delivered equipment without obtaining ADASA approval of the corresponding datasheets, in violation of the review process established in ET Section 7:

| Equipment | Status | Non-Compliance |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Static Mixer | Manufactured Oct-Nov 2025 | DS Rev A verdict "3 - To be revised" (material PVC vs FRP). Equipment manufactured before resolution. |
| Electrical Panel / MCC | Delivered Jan 2026 | MCC Datasheet never submitted to ADASA. Equipment fabricated and delivered without any ADASA review. |
| HP Pump | PR/PO scheduled Feb 16, 2026 | DS Rev B verdict "3 - To be revised". Missing Pt-100 sensors, inconsistent power ratings. |

### 3.3 Failure to Respond to Formal Communications

| Communication | Date Sent | Response Status |
|---------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Transmittal N3 (P22-TM-09-000-003-0) | 28-Jan-2026 | **No response** |
| Transmittal N4 (P22-TM-09-000-004-0) | 05-Feb-2026 | **No response** |
| Technical Query CT-001 (Antiscalant dosing) | 05-Feb-2026 | **No response** (deadline 10-Feb - OVERDUE) |
| Schedule Review and Requirements (10-Feb-2026) | 10-Feb-2026 | **No response** (deadline 13-Feb) |

### 3.4 Missing Contractual Activities in Project Schedule

BW Water's updated schedule (February 2026) omits the following contractual obligations:
- Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)
- Site Supervision and Commissioning
- Operator Training
- Final Documentation and Close-out
- Document delivery schedule

Additionally, the schedule shows shipping to Florida instead of making the module available EXW at the manufacturing facility in Malaysia, which is inconsistent with the EXW delivery term under Contract C-4300.

## 5. REQUIRED REMEDIATION ACTIONS

In accordance with BAE Clause 49, BW Water is required to remedy the identified non-compliance within **ten (10) calendar days** from receipt of this notification. Specifically, ADASA requires:

1. **Immediate submission** of a document delivery schedule with specific dates for all 14 undelivered documents and all 16 documents requiring revision.

2. **Immediate submission** of the MCC Datasheet for retroactive verification of the electrical panel already fabricated.

3. **Formal responses** to Transmittals N3 and N4, including action plans for all observations marked as "To be revised."

4. **Response to Technical Query CT-001** (antiscalant dosing), overdue since February 10, 2026.

5. **Updated project schedule** incorporating all contractual milestones (FAT, Commissioning, Training, Close-out) and clarification of the EXW delivery point and date.

6. **Confirmation** that no purchase orders will be issued for equipment whose datasheets have not been approved by ADASA.

7. **Submission** of the Modbus TCP Memory Map and the A/C n+1 thermal calculation, both committed in ADASA's Transmittal N2 response (January 26, 2026).

8. **Engineering recovery plan** demonstrating how BW Water intends to minimize the projected 109-day delay.

## 6. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-REMEDIATION

BW Water is required to **acknowledge receipt** of this notification and inform ADASA without delay regarding the effects of the identified non-compliance and the corrective measures to be implemented (BAE Clause 49).

If BW Water fails to remedy the identified non-compliance within the 10-day cure period, ADASA may exercise any or all of the following rights under the Contract:

**Under BAE Clause 49:**
- Impose ADASA's technical assistance on BW Water, at BW Water's cost
- Substitute BW Water, in whole or in part, at BW Water's risk and expense (including a 15% surcharge for administrative costs per BAE Clause 49)

**Under BAE Clause 50:**
- Early termination of the Contract, without incurring additional costs, on the grounds of:
  - Contractual non-compliance as established in Clause 49
  - Manifest inability of the Supplier to deliver according to specifications and schedule

**Under BAE Clause 43:**
- Continued automatic accrual of delay penalties
- Deduction of penalties from pending payments or execution of the Performance Bond (Boleta de Garantia de Fiel Cumplimiento)
