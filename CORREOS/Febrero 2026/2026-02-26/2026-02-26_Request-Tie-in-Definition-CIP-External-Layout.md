**Paragraph 2 — Main process tie-ins:**

For the design of the piping interface between the ADASA plant and the RO module, the following process connection points are required at firme: feed inlet (brine), permeate outlet, and concentrate/reject outlet. For each connection, ADASA requires: nominal diameter, flange standard and rating, container face where the nozzle emerges, and centerline elevation referenced to the bottom floor of the container (elevation 0.00). The revised layout shall confirm that all process connections are concentrated on the designated face, consistent with ADASA's annotation regarding process infrastructure alignment.

**Paragraph 3 — New tie-ins generated by external CIP and antiscalant:**

Relocating the CIP and antiscalant dosing equipment outside the container — per Offer Rev.1 and the February 18 agreement — generates additional connection points that must be defined at firme: CIP feed (from internal permeate header), CIP return to Stage 1, CIP return to Stage 2, CIP reject/drain, and antiscalant injection point in the feed line upstream of the cartridge filter. For each connection: nominal diameter, fitting or flange type, container face, and centerline elevation referenced to elevation 0.00.

**Paragraph 4 — External CIP footprint:**

To design the external foundation and platform where the CIP equipment will be installed — also requested in TM N5 OBS-01 — ADASA requires a preliminary layout or dimensioned sketch showing the footprint of: CIP Tank (reference: 71" D × 88" H per Offer Rev.1, approx. 1.8 m × 2.2 m, 4,498 kg when full), CIP Pump skid, CIP Cartridge Filter, and Antiscalant dosing skid. Minimum maintenance clearances must be indicated. An IFC drawing is not required at this stage; a preliminary sketch committed at firme is sufficient. I can share the Taltal site plan to facilitate coordination of the external CIP location.

## Contexto Interno (No enviar)

### Propósito estratégico

Este correo es de **contingencia técnica**: no presiona por el plano revisado completo (ese es el proceso de TM N5), sino que pide la información mínima para desbloquear la ingeniería periférica de ADASA. El tono es técnico y específico, sin escalamiento contractual.

### Por qué ahora

- La ingeniería civil y de piping de interconexión depende de conocer las elevaciones de los nozzles del container. Sin esos datos, ADASA no puede dimensionar la zanja, el rack de tuberías ni las soporterías.
- El footprint del CIP externo afecta el diseño de la losa/plataforma de ADASA. Si BW Water no entrega el footprint, ADASA tendrá que asumir dimensiones y arriesgarse a una reingeniería costosa.
- Cada semana de espera retrasa la ingeniería civil, que es ruta crítica hacia FAT (julio 2026).
