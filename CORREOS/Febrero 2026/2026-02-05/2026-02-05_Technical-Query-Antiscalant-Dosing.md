Dear BW Water Team,

We are submitting Technical Query P22-CT-09-000-001-0 regarding the antiscalant dosing system.

During our detailed review, we noted that the Chemical Consumption List specifies an antiscalant dosage of 0.5 ppm. Based on the LG Chem membrane projections in your Technical Offer, the concentrate LSI reaches 2.0-2.11 at worst-case conditions (TDS 53,000 ppm, 24C). Industry practice for applications with LSI above 2.0 typically requires dosing rates between 5-10 ppm.

We want to clarify that the referenced documents (P22-LI-09-009-002-A and P22-ET-09-009-004-B) were approved in Transmittal N3 (P22-TM-09-000-003-0). We acknowledge positive aspects of your design: the dosing pump has a capacity margin of 115x (allowing dosage adjustments without equipment replacement), and the tank capacity exceeds the Technical Offer requirement.

Please provide the antiscalant manufacturer's scaling projection or simulation demonstrating that 0.5 ppm is sufficient for brine concentration service with the specified feed water quality (TDS up to 53,000 ppm, LSI 2.0-2.11). Los documentos de dosificacion de antiescalante fueron **aprobados en TM N3** (P22-LI-09-009-002-A y P22-ET-09-009-004-B). La Consulta Tecnica es el mecanismo adecuado para solicitar documentacion de soporte sin modificar el veredicto de documentos ya aprobados.

**Actualizacion TM N4:** El Antiscalant Dosing Tank (P22-ET-09-009-010-B) se aprueba con nota (OBS-14) indicando que la aprobacion esta **sujeta a respuesta satisfactoria a esta Technical Query**. Esto asegura coherencia entre:
- Aprobar el EQUIPO (tanque) como componente fisico
- Cuestionar los PARAMETROS del sistema (dosis 0.5 ppm)

La TQ se emite simultaneamente con el TM N4 para mantener trazabilidad.

### Analisis Tecnico

| Parametro | Valor | Comentario |
|-----------|-------|------------|
| Dosis especificada | 0.5 ppm | 10-20x menor que tipico |

| Margen bomba | 115x | **Positivo** - permite ajustes |

### Riesgo de Scaling

El LG Chem disclaimer transfiere la responsabilidad de proteccion contra scaling a BW Water:
> "It is the user's responsibility to make provisions against fouling, scaling and chemical attacks."

Si las membranas fallan por scaling, BW Water es responsable.

### Posibles Respuestas de BW Water

1. **Proporcionan proyeccion del fabricante:** Aceptable si demuestra eficacia a 0.5 ppm
2. **No pueden justificar:** Deberian proponer dosis mayor y actualizar lista de consumos
3. **Dicen que es practica estandar:** Solicitar documentacion que lo respalde
