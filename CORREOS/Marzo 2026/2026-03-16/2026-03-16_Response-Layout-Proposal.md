### Regarding the Seven Documents

ADASA accepts that the following four documents require revision to reflect the corrected CIP configuration: P22-DWG-09-005-004 (Piping Layout), P22-DWG-09-005-005 (Tie-In Points), the Equipment Layout, and the 3D Model. We are prepared to include these in the accepted scope.

For the following three documents, ADASA cannot confirm the technical dependency without additional justification:

1. **Instrument Layout (P22-DWG-09-008-001)**
2. **Grounding Point & Power Panel Layout (P22-DWG-09-007-003)**
3. **Cable Tray Layout (P22-DWG-09-007-004)**

These documents cover electrical and instrument systems located inside the module container. The CIP system is being relocated outside the container. The relocation of an external system does not, in itself, create an evident impact on internal cable tray routing, grounding layout, or instrument arrangement within the enclosure.

Cable Tray Layout P22-DWG-09-007-004 Rev A was reviewed and approved without a single observation in Transmittal N4, Code 1. The same applies to the Instrument Layout and Grounding Layout. ADASA is not ruling out that a design dependency may exist — but before including these documents in the accepted scope, we ask BW Water to provide a brief technical justification tracing the specific dependency between the external CIP relocation and each of these three internal-module drawings, or alternatively to remove them from the proposal.

### ADASA's Position

ADASA recognizes that this work must proceed and we are not seeking to block or delay it. We accept the proposal in principle and intend to move forward once we receive the updated proposal described below.

We document the following for the record, so that both parties have a clear and agreed understanding of how this situation arose:

The Technical Offer Rev.1 confirmed that the CIP system would be located outside the module container — a requirement unchanged since contract award. ADASA established a maximum connection distance of 3.5 m from the module boundary through Transmittal N5 (P22-TM-09-000-005-1) on February 23, 2026, two weeks before BW Water submitted the Piping Layout. That constraint was in writing. The delivered Piping Layout placed CIP connections at 11,150 mm — more than three times the specified ceiling. The layout was also delivered 66 calendar days past the contractual deadline, after ADASA had already issued a formal written reminder with a specific deadline that passed without response.

The revisions now required are a direct consequence of a non-conforming delivery that arrived after a documented, written constraint was already on record. This documentation preserves both parties' contractual positions and is intended as a factual reference, not as an obstacle to proceeding.

### What We Are Asking

**We require BW Water's updated proposal by end of business today, March 16, 2026.**

This is not a new design exercise — it is an update to the proposal already submitted. We are asking for three specific items:

**(a)** For the three internal-module documents (Instrument Layout, Grounding Layout, Cable Tray Layout): either a brief technical justification tracing the specific design dependency between the external CIP relocation and each drawing, or their removal from the proposal scope.

**(b)** A revised timeline integrated with the overall engineering recovery plan and the FAT schedule.

**(c)** Commercial terms that reflect the delivery history and the origin of the non-conformance documented above.

**If we do not receive the updated proposal today, ADASA will proceed by end of business today with a conditional acceptance covering only the four documents with evident technical dependency (Piping Layout, Tie-In Points, Equipment Layout, 3D Model).** Acceptance of the three internal-module documents will remain on hold until the technical justification is received.
