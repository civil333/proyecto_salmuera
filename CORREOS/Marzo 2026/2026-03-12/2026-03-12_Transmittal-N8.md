**Instrument List Rev B — acknowledged progress:**
Rev B adds the three vibration transmitters (VT-09-001/002/003) and four motor RTDs (TE-09-001/002/003/004) requested in previous transmittals. These additions close two long-standing technical gaps and are acknowledged.

**Instrument List Rev B — blocking item (Code 3):**
CIT-09-005 (RO Train Reject Conductivity Analyzer) specifies 120VAC power supply. Every other instrument in the list operates at 24VDC. BW Water must confirm whether 220VAC was the intended supply voltage (correct the Instrument List accordingly) or provide the single-line diagram for the 120VAC distribution circuit. IO List Rev B must also be submitted incorporating the seven new instruments added in this revision.

**Brine-side conductivity ranges — confirmation required:**
CIT-09-001 (module feed), CIT-09-004 (Stage 1 reject), and CIT-09-005 (train reject) specify a maximum calibrated range of 20 mS/cm. Based on the design feed TDS of 43,000–53,000 mg/L, expected conductivities at these three points exceed 65 mS/cm. BW Water must provide measured conductivity values for the Taltal brine and correct the calibrated ranges in Rev C if the 20 mS/cm limit is insufficient.

**Power Works Installation Drawing Rev A — no grounding specifications (Code 3):**
The document contains no equipment earth conductors, no cable tray bonding requirements, and no shield termination guidance for instrument cables. Rev B must include grounding specifications for motors, panels, cable trays, and instrument cables.

**Temperature Transmitter Datasheet Rev A — TIT-09-003 absent from Instrument List (Code 3):**
The datasheet covers TIT-09-003, a tag not registered in Instrument List Rev B and with no IO point assigned. BW Water must add TIT-09-003 to the next IL revision and confirm whether TIT-09-001 and TIT-09-003 are distinct instruments or one supersedes the other.
